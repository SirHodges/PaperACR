Ambulance Call Report

Frequently asked questions

Updates and answers for the team.

What do I do if I run out of space in the Clinical Treatment / Procedures section?

Start a second ACR. Strike through the front page of the second ACR with a single line and initial it, then make sure the Call # and page numbers are recorded accurately on both forms.

Do I have to enter the call number on the second page?

Yes — the call number must be recorded on both pages of the ACR.

Can I enter "Transfer" or "MVA (MVC)" in the Chief Complaint area?

No. The Chief Complaint should reflect the patient's expressed issue — in the patient's own words whenever possible. A mechanism of injury (like an MVC) isn't a complaint, so it doesn't belong in this section.

How do I know which code goes in Primary Problem vs. Secondary Problem?

Choose the codes that best represent the patient's primary issue. They're listed on the back of the ACR, and the complete list of ministry problem codes is available on ontario.ca.

Should I be transmitting my defib data?

Yes, please — keep uploading it every time. The data uploads to Stryker, who hold it for us for up to 30 days.

Why do we need to stop the SMART program for now?

Without digital forms, we have no way to reliably follow up with patients, so sadly, we need to pause the program.

Should Operations continue to email referrals to CP while on paper ACRs?

Yes, continue to submit your referrals to CommunityParamedicine@Ottawa.ca with the call number (no PHI please) and CP will process them as we work through filing.

Why do we need to stop the studies?

We can't continue studies if we can't reliably capture the data contemporaneously. Epidose packets are being pulled from the kits to make it less confusing on hot calls. We'll update you again when we can go live!
